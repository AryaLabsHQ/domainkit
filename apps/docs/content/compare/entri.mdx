## Verdict

**Choose Entri** if you want a managed, white-labelled flow, coverage across many DNS providers,
and a vendor to call.

**Choose DomainKit** if your customers are mostly on Cloudflare or Vercel, you want the flow inside
your own app and database, and you want each customer to approve an exact plan.

## What Entri does well

Entri covers far more DNS providers than DomainKit does. If your customers use Namecheap, GoDaddy,
Route 53, Squarespace, or Porkbun, Entri's list includes them and DomainKit does not.

Entri is a finished, hosted flow. You mint a token, call one function, and get a modal that detects
the provider, signs the customer in, and applies the records. It has a webhook for when records
resolve. Entri also sells the pieces around it, such as SSL and root-domain hosting.

Entri's site shows Webflow and Twilio as customers. If a vendor SLA and a name your security team
recognizes matter to you, Entri answers both.

## What DomainKit does differently

DomainKit is a library, so the flow lives in your app. Your database holds the connections, your
key seals the credentials, and your session decides who can approve. There is no separate vendor
holding those tokens.

The customer approves an exact plan. Each record is marked Will add, Already set, or Conflict
before anything is written, and the approval covers that plan's digest. If the zone changes in
between, apply stops.

DomainKit keeps a receipt of each write and plans cleanup from it, so removing records later is as
reviewed as adding them was.

It costs nothing per domain. The cost is your own engineering time to integrate and run it.

## Choose which

### Choose Entri when

- Your customers are spread across many registrars.
- You want a hosted flow live quickly, without writing server routes or storage.
- You want a vendor with a support contract.
- You also need domain purchase, SSL, or root-domain hosting from the same vendor.

### Choose DomainKit when

- Most of your customers use Cloudflare or Vercel for DNS.
- You want credentials and rows in your own database.
- You want the customer to review and approve an exact plan, with a receipt.
- You want the flow to look and behave like the rest of your product.

## Using both

The two aren't exclusive. Some teams will use DomainKit for customers on Cloudflare or Vercel and a
hosted service for the rest. DomainKit doesn't integrate with Entri, so you choose the path in your
own UI.

## Questions to ask any vendor

- Where are my customers' provider credentials stored, and for how long?
- What does my customer see before a record is written?
- What happens when a record already exists at that name?
- Which providers are covered today, and how is that list maintained?
- What happens to my customers' setup if I leave?

## FAQ

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title="Is DomainKit an Entri alternative?">
    It solves the same problem, letting customers connect a domain without copying DNS records, for
    a smaller set of providers and with a different model. DomainKit is a library you run. Entri is
    a service you call.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Does DomainKit cost anything?">
    No. It's MIT-licensed with no per-domain fee. Entri's published price starts at $249 a month for
    the Startup plan, with higher tiers by quote.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Does DomainKit cover my customers' registrars?">
    Only Cloudflare and Vercel today. Entri's site says 70+ providers. Check your customers' DNS
    providers against both lists before you decide.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Can I use DomainKit for domain purchase or SSL?">
    No. DomainKit writes DNS records. For certificates and traffic, see [Cloudflare for
    SaaS](/compare/cloudflare-for-saas), Vercel, or a proxy service.
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
