Domain Connect is a protocol. A service provider publishes a DNS template, a supporting DNS
provider renders consent for it, and the customer applies it in the DNS provider's own interface.

DomainKit is a library your application runs. It connects a provider through that provider's API,
builds a plan from the records your product needs, records approval for that exact plan, applies
it, keeps a receipt, and observes DNS afterwards.

## What DomainKit does not do

DomainKit does not implement Domain Connect. There is no Domain Connect provider adapter, no
template publishing, and no plan to describe DomainKit as a replacement for the protocol. That work
is parked, not in progress.

If your product's setup is a fixed template on a DNS provider that already supports Domain Connect
and hosts your template, the protocol does that job without a library.

## What DomainKit does instead

DomainKit is built for the setup that continues after the first click:

- The record values differ per customer, per tenant, or per environment.
- The product needs to see whether the records are actually live, from the provider and from public
  DNS, and to say when to look again.
- The same provider access has to serve the customer's next domain.
- Removing records later has to be as reviewed as adding them was.

Those are the pieces on [plans](/docs/core/plans),
[connections](/docs/core/connections), [verification](/docs/core/verification), and
[cleanup](/docs/core/cleanup).
